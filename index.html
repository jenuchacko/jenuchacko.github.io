Jenu Varghese Chacko

Scientist · Optics and Imaging

Cell Imaging · Laboratory for Optical and Computational Instrumentation (LOCI) · University of Wisconsin–Madison

I specialize in optical instrumentation and data-driven biomedical imaging, including microscope development and autofluorescence techniques for assessing tissue metabolism.

Email · GitHub · LinkedIn · ORCID · PubMed · Publications on Google Scholar · Peer review record

Education

  • 2011–2014 · PhD in NanoSciences, University of Genoa and Italian Institute of Technology, Genoa, Italy. With Benjamin Harke and Alberto Diaspro.
  • 2004–2009 · Integrated MSc in Photonics, Cochin University of Science and Technology, Cochin, India. Five-year integrated master's degree.

Research appointments

  • 2017–present · University of Wisconsin–Madison, LOCI and Cell and Molecular Biology. Scientist II (2019–present); Postdoctoral Scholar in Quantitative Cell Imaging (2017–2019). With Kevin Eliceiri.
  • 2014–2017 · Postdoctoral Scholar in Biomedical Engineering, Biomedical Engineering and Laboratory for Fluorescence Dynamics, University of California, Irvine. With Michelle Digman and Enrico Gratton.
  • 2009–2010 · Research Fellow in Microscopy, Tata Institute of Fundamental Research, Mumbai, India. Supervised by Sudipta Maiti.

Methods and software

  • Fluorescence: TR-anisotropy, FLIM, FCS, iPCF, iMSD, PCF, N&B and ICS.
  • Imaging: multiphoton and confocal microscopy, STED, AFM, STORM/PALM, SHG, THG and Raman.
  • Optics: 3D orbital tracking, W-FLIM, OPO and MIRA/Tsunami.
  • Programming: Python, Java, C/C++, JavaScript and MATLAB.
