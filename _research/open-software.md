Lifetime analysis is only as trustworthy as the software and calibration behind it. I contribute to open FLIM analysis tools and to community efforts on quality control.

## Software

- [flimj-ops](https://github.com/flimlib/flimj-ops): ImageJ Ops plugins for fluorescence lifetime image analysis.
- [Decay ↔ phasor explorer](/interactive/phasor-explorer.html): an in-browser teaching tool for phasor analysis.

## Methods

- [Nonparametric empirical Bayesian framework for fluorescence-lifetime imaging microscopy](https://doi.org/10.1364/BOE.10.005497). *Biomed. Opt. Express* 10, 2019.
- [Fluorescence lifetime: techniques, analysis, and applications in the life sciences](https://www.routledge.com/Imaging-from-Cells-to-Animals-In-Vivo/Barroso-Intes/p/book/9781138041097). Book chapter in *Imaging from Cells to Animals In Vivo*, 2020.
